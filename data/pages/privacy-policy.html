At HusnainBlogger (husnainblogger.com), your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we do and don't collect when you use our website, and how we handle it. In short: our tools run entirely in your browser, so we don't collect or store the things you type into them.

If you have questions about this policy, reach us anytime at husnainblogger.com@gmail.com.

This policy applies only to our website and its online activities. It does not cover information collected offline or through other channels.

Consent

By using HusnainBlogger, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

What our tools do with your input

HusnainBlogger offers free tools that run 100% client-side — the calculations, conversions, and transformations happen inside your own browser on your own device. Nothing you enter into a tool is sent to our servers, stored by us, or shared with anyone. When you close the tab, that data is gone.

Information we may collect

  • No accounts, no sign-ups. We don't ask you to register, and we don't store usernames, passwords, or profiles — because they don't exist on this site.
  • If you contact us. When you email us at husnainblogger.com@gmail.com, we receive whatever you choose to include (your name, email address, message, and any attachments). We use that only to reply to you.
  • Server logs. Like nearly every website, our hosting records standard log data — your IP address, browser type, device type, date and time of visit, referring/exit pages, and pages viewed. This information is not linked to anything that personally identifies you; we use it only to keep the site running, detect abuse, and understand overall usage trends.
  • Analytics. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to see which tools are popular. This uses aggregate, non-identifying data.

How we use this information

  • To operate, maintain, and secure the website
  • To understand which tools people use so we can improve them
  • To respond to your messages and support requests
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

We don't sell your personal information. We don't send marketing emails, because we don't collect email addresses in the first place (unless you email us yourself).

Cookies

HusnainBlogger uses a small number of cookies — mainly to remember your preferences and, when advertising is enabled, to help show relevant ads. You can read the full details in our Cookies Policy.

Advertising

Our advertising slots are currently turned off. If we enable display advertising (such as Google AdSense) in the future, third-party ad networks may use cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons to measure campaign performance and personalize ads. They receive your IP address when their ads load, and their use of your data is governed by their own privacy policies — we have no access to or control over those cookies.

Google may use the DoubleClick cookie (DART) to serve interest-based ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and manage Google ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Third-party websites

Our tools and articles may link to other websites. We have no control over their content or privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing them any information.

Your data protection rights

Because we don't maintain accounts or store tool inputs, there is typically no personal data of yours for us to hand over, correct, or delete — but your rights still matter:

  • Access and erasure (GDPR / CCPA): you may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you (for example, messages you sent us) and ask us to delete it.
  • Do not sell (CCPA): we do not sell personal information, full stop.
  • Rectification, restriction, objection, and portability (GDPR): these apply under the usual conditions where we process your data.

If you make a request, we'll respond within one month. Contact: husnainblogger.com@gmail.com.

Children's information

Protecting children online is important to us. HusnainBlogger does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child provided personal information here (for example, by emailing us), please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect as soon as they're posted on this page, so we encourage you to review it periodically. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.

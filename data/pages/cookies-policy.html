HusnainBlogger (husnainblogger.com) uses cookies — and this page explains what they are, which ones we use, and how you can control them. By using the site, you consent to the use of cookies as described here.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They help the site remember things about your visit — like your preferences — and they help us understand how the site is used.

Cookies can be "persistent" (they stay on your device after you go offline, until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (deleted automatically when you close your browser).

How HusnainBlogger uses cookies

We keep cookie use minimal. When cookies are set, they're for:

  • Essential operation: cookies needed for the site to function properly.
  • Preferences: remembering your choices (such as display settings) so the site feels familiar on your next visit.
  • Analytics: understanding which tools are popular and how visitors navigate the site, using aggregate, non-identifying data.
  • Advertising (when enabled): our ad slots are currently off. If we enable display advertising in the future, ad networks may set their own cookies to measure campaign performance and show relevant ads.

We don't use cookies to collect sensitive personal data, and we don't use them to track you across unrelated websites.

Third-party cookies

In addition to our own cookies, third-party services we use — such as analytics providers or, in the future, advertising networks — may set their own cookies. Their cookies are governed by those third parties' own privacy policies, not ours. See our Privacy Policy for details on advertising partners.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings — every major browser lets you block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or clear existing ones. Keep in mind that blocking cookies may affect how the site works.

For advertising cookies specifically, you can opt out of personalized ads through Google's ad settings and the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.
